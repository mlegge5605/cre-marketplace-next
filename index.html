This tool is designed to help you understand your own portfolio, not to sell you products.

What we found

Educational estimates based on available statement and account data. Not personalized investment advice.

Risk
Your portfolio may be more aggressive than your comfort level

Based on available data, your combined portfolio holds approximately 72% in equities, which is consistent with a Growth to Aggressive Growth profile. You've indicated a comfort level of Balanced Growth, which typically suggests a 55–65% equity allocation.

Why this matters
If markets experience a significant correction, a portfolio more aggressive than your comfort level may cause stress or lead to poorly-timed decisions like selling at a loss.
Consider reviewing your target allocation with a financial professional.
Diversification
You may have a high Canadian equity concentration

An estimated 58% of your equity holdings appear to be in Canadian-listed securities. Canada represents roughly 3–4% of the global equity market, so a significant home-country bias may limit diversification.

Why this matters
Over-concentration in one geography can increase volatility relative to a globally diversified portfolio and reduce exposure to growth opportunities in other markets.
Consider reviewing geographic diversification in your equity allocation.
Fees
Estimated fees appear higher than some lower-cost alternatives

Your estimated weighted average fee of 1.15% annually (~$4,920/year) includes several actively managed mutual funds with MERs between 1.7% and 2.3%. Comparable index ETFs often carry fees of 0.07–0.25%.

Why this matters
Over 20 years, a 1% fee difference on a $400,000 portfolio can compound to over $80,000 in additional costs. This is an educational estimate only — fees are one factor among many to consider.
Consider reviewing whether current funds align with your goals and risk tolerance.
Overlap
Several funds may have overlapping holdings

Based on fund descriptions and available holdings data, your Sun Life RRSP and Wealthsimple TFSA appear to hold similar Canadian equity and balanced funds. This may result in unintentional concentration in certain securities.

Why this matters
Holding multiple funds with similar mandates can create the appearance of diversification while actually concentrating your exposure. A full overlap analysis requires complete holdings data.
Consider requesting a holdings report from each institution for a complete review.
Time horizon
Your RESP may be too conservative for its time horizon

Your Manulife RESP ($22,700) appears to be invested primarily in a conservative balanced fund. If the beneficiary is under age 10, there may be 8+ years of growth runway available, which can support a more growth-oriented allocation early on.

Why this matters
RESP time-to-use is a key factor in determining appropriate risk. Many professionals suggest gradually reducing equity exposure as the beneficiary approaches post-secondary enrollment age.
Consider reviewing your RESP investment strategy relative to the beneficiary's expected enrollment date.
Data quality
Some fee data is missing from external statements

Fee information could not be extracted for approximately $62,000 of your holdings, primarily from the Questrade Margin account. This means the 1.15% weighted average fee estimate may be understated.

Why this matters
Incomplete fee data limits the accuracy of the portfolio fee analysis. Including a more detailed statement or granting read-only account access would improve insight quality.
Upload a more recent or detailed Questrade statement to improve accuracy.
